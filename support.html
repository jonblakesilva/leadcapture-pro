LeadCapture Pro Chrome Extension  ·  Version 1.7.6

Getting Started

LeadCapture Pro detects business contact information on any web page and lets you push it directly to your GoHighLevel CRM. To get set up:

  • Open the extension popup and click Open Settings
  • Paste your GHL Private Integration API key (150+ characters)
  • Paste your GHL Location ID from your account URL
  • Click Test Connection to verify
  • Browse any page with a business phone number — the blue button appears automatically
Your API key is stored locally in your browser via chrome.storage.sync. It is never sent anywhere except directly to GoHighLevel.

Common Issues

The blue button doesn't appear on a page
The extension only shows the button when it detects a phone number or email address on the page. Try scrolling down — some contact info loads dynamically. On Google Maps, wait 2–3 seconds after the business panel opens. If it still doesn't appear, the page may not have detectable contact data.
GHL rejected the contact with a 400 error
This usually means the contact already exists in GHL. Click Check GHL first — if a match is found, use Update instead of Push. If the contact is truly new, verify the phone number has exactly 10 digits and the business name field is filled in.
"Invalid API key" or 401 error
Go to GHL → Settings → Integrations → Private Integrations and confirm your key is active. The key should be 150+ characters. Make sure you copied the full key with no extra spaces. Also verify all 5 required scopes are enabled: contacts.readonly, contacts.write, opportunities.readonly, opportunities.write, locations.readonly.
Where do I find my Location ID?
Log into your GHL account. Look at the URL — it will contain something like /location/6B4reCngPYfDkJwOtiM0/. That long string between /location/ and the next / is your Location ID.
Contact data is missing (no address or website)
On Google Maps, the address and website load a second or two after the business name. The extension automatically retries 3 times (at 0.8s, 2s, and 4s) to capture the full data. If data is still missing after a few seconds, click the blue button to open the sidebar — you can fill in any missing fields manually before pushing.
The sidebar won't close
Click the ✕ button at the top right of the sidebar panel. If that doesn't work, clicking the blue floating button again will toggle the sidebar closed.
How do I update the extension?
Chrome updates extensions automatically. To force an update immediately, go to chrome://extensions, enable Developer Mode (top right), then click Update.
