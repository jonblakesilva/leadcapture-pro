Log in to your GHL sub-account

Create a new integration

FAQ & Troubleshooting

My connection test fails — what do I check?

Make sure you're using a Private Integration key (not an OAuth token). Go to GHL → Settings → Integrations → Private Integrations. The key should be 150+ characters. Also confirm all 5 scopes are enabled: contacts.readonly, contacts.write, opportunities.readonly, opportunities.write, locations.readonly.

The Push button is greyed out.

A phone number or email must be present to push. If none was detected automatically, scroll down in the sidebar and use Manual Entry to add one.

The contact shows as a duplicate in GHL.

LeadCapture checks for existing contacts before pushing. If a match is found, click Update to merge the new data into the existing record without creating a duplicate.

Contacts are pushed but not appearing in my pipeline.

Set a Default Pipeline and Stage in Step 3. If you've already set it, check that your API key has the opportunities.write scope enabled.

The sidebar doesn't appear on some websites.

Some sites use aggressive Content Security Policies that block injected scripts. Try refreshing the page. The extension also doesn't run on Chrome Web Store pages or internal Chrome URLs.

Address and website aren't showing for Google Maps listings.

Google Maps loads data in stages. LeadCapture automatically retries after 1.5 seconds. If data is still missing, close and reopen the sidebar — the latest data will load.

🛡️ Objection Handlers — NEPQ Framework

From Jeremy Miner's NEPQ (Neuro-Emotional Persuasion Questions). Never defend, never push back — ask a question that creates self-awareness. The prospect convinces themselves.

🧠 The rule: never pitch, never defend. Respond to every objection with a softened, curious question. Silence after the question is your friend.
"I already work with someone."
NEPQ — Problem Awareness
"That's great — how long have you been working with them? And how do you feel like things are going with your online presence right now — like your Google reviews and the number of calls you're getting from search?"
Goal: get them to voice their own dissatisfaction. If they're happy, they're not your prospect. If they hedge, keep asking.
"Not interested."
NEPQ — Consequence Question
"Completely understand. Can I ask — is it more that business is already great and you don't need more customers right now, or is it that you've tried things like this before and it didn't pan out?"
This reframes "not interested" as a solvable problem. Most say the second one — then you have a conversation.
"Just send me some information."
NEPQ — Clarifying Question
"Of course — I want to send you something actually useful. Quick question before I do: is most of your new business coming from Google and online, or more from referrals and word of mouth right now?"
Gets them talking before they ghost your email. Referral-heavy = they need online presence. Google-heavy = they care about rankings.
"How much does it cost?"
NEPQ — Future Pace
"That's a fair question and I want to give you a real answer — but it depends on what's actually going on with your online presence. What would it mean for your business if you were getting 5–10 extra calls a month from Google?"
Never answer price before value. Their answer reveals ROI tolerance — then you price to that number.
"I don't have time right now."
NEPQ — Low Friction
"Totally get it — no call needed at all. I recorded a 2-minute video for you — just shows what I found on your listing. You can watch it whenever. Want me to send the link?"
Turn every stall into a video request. Video-first is 2026 — never ask for time before they've seen value.
"We tried marketing before and it didn't work."
NEPQ — Past Experience
"That's really common — and honestly, most marketing agencies sell the same thing. What was it specifically that didn't deliver? Was it the leads weren't qualified, or more that you didn't see enough volume to judge it?"
Identify the real failure point. This separates you from whoever burned them before and positions your approach as different.

🔥 Warm Outreach — Relationship Pipeline

Cold outreach gets you the first conversation. Warm outreach is how you build the pipeline that pays you 12 months from now. Based on Alex Hormozi's relationship-first offer framework.

📊 Gary Vee's Jab Framework + Hormozi's Grand Slam Offer

Gary Vaynerchuk coined "Jab Jab Jab Right Hook" — give value three times before you ask. Alex Hormozi refined the ask itself: make your offer so good it's almost stupid to say no (the Grand Slam Offer). Use both together.

1
Jab 1 — Free Audit Video (Loom)
Unsolicited. No ask. Just send it. "I recorded this for you — no strings attached." This is the highest-converting cold touch you can send. Costs 3 minutes. (Gary Vee: the first jab)
2
Jab 2 — Free Quick Win (Deliver First)
If they reply, do one small thing for free — write them a better Google Business description, fix a review response, suggest 1 keyword fix. Proof before pitch. (Gary Vee: the second jab)
3
Jab 3 — Value-Add Check-in
A tip, a resource, or a "noticed this about your competitor" touch. No ask yet. At this point they know you deliver, not just pitch. (Gary Vee: the third jab)
4
Right Hook — The Grand Slam Offer (Hormozi)
Now you ask. But with a Hormozi Grand Slam Offer: "[Dream Outcome] in [Timeframe], or [Guarantee]. Done for you. Only [N] spots this month." Stack value so high the price feels obvious. Dream outcome + high likelihood + fast timeline + zero effort for them = irresistible.

📱 Social Media Engagement Strategy

Warm them up before you DM. Takes 5 minutes per prospect. Dramatically increases reply rates on cold DMs.

👤
Step 1 — Follow + Connect (Day 1)
Follow their business on Instagram. Send a LinkedIn connect request — no note. Friend request on Facebook if it's a personal profile. Don't say anything yet.
❤️
Step 2 — Engage Authentically (Day 2–3)
Like 2–3 recent posts. Leave one genuine comment — not "Great post!" but something specific: "That before/after on the [job type] is impressive — how long did that take?" Shows you actually looked.
💬
Step 3 — DM After Warm-Up (Day 3–4)
Now you're not a stranger. Open with a callback to your comment: "Hey — I left a comment on your [post]. I also noticed something about your Google listing I thought was worth mentioning..." See Cold DM templates above.
🎥
Step 4 — Video First, Call Never (until they ask)
Send the Loom link in DM #2 only if they reply to DM #1. Never ask for a call in a cold DM — you haven't earned it. "Want me to send the video?" is the only ask. Let the video do the pitching.
