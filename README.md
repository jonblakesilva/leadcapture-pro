# LeadCapture Pro

**Push any business lead into GoHighLevel in one click — from any website.**

---

## What Is LeadCapture Pro?

LeadCapture Pro is a Chrome extension built for marketing agencies, GHL agencies, and anyone who needs to build lead lists fast.

Browse any website. The extension detects phone numbers, emails, business names, and addresses automatically. Hit push. The contact lands in your GoHighLevel CRM — tagged, staged, and ready for outreach.

No copy-paste. No tab switching. No manual data entry.

---

## The Core Problem It Solves

Agencies waste 2–4 hours per day manually copying business info from websites into their CRM.

LeadCapture Pro eliminates that entirely.

- **Find** a business on Google Maps, Yelp, BBB, or anywhere
- **Click** the extension

That's it. The contact is in your pipeline with tags, source info, and a note — ready for automated outreach.

## Features

### Lead Capture
- **Auto-detects** phones, emails, addresses, and business names from any page structure
- **Schema.org support** — reads structured JSON-LD data for high-accuracy extraction
- **Confidence scoring** — green (tel: link / structured data), yellow (page text), red (low confidence)
- **Batch capture** — detect multiple contacts on a directory page and push them all at once

### CRM Integration
- **One-click push** to GoHighLevel — creates contact, tags it, stages it, enrolls in workflow
- **Duplicate detection** — checks GHL before pushing so you never create dupes
- **Pipeline + stage** selection at push time
- **GHL workflow enrollment** — trigger automations the moment a lead is pushed
- **Auto notes** — every contact gets a capture note with source URL, date, review data, and marketing score

### Lead Intelligence (Google Maps)
- **Review count + star rating** pulled automatically
- **Marketing score (0–100)** computed at push time based on website presence, phone, email, review count, and rating
- Tells you instantly which leads have weak marketing (= easiest to sell to)
- **No-website filter** — one click shows only businesses without a website
- **Bad reviews filter** — filter for businesses rated under 4 stars
- **No-phone filter** — surface contacts missing a phone number

### Video Audit Tool
- **Record Audit Video button** — appears on any contact with a website
- One click opens the prospect's site in a new tab + Loom recorder, ready to record
- **Auto-generated talking points** — based on detected signals (no website, bad reviews, no email, etc.)
- **8-point audit checklist** — walks through mobile, speed, SEO, reviews, social, CTA, local listings, and branding

### Outreach Templates (Settings → Step 5)
- **Cold email template** — copy in one click, personalized for Loom audit workflow
- **SMS template** — short, direct intro for text outreach
- **Follow-up email** — 3-day follow-up sequence template
- **GHL workflow overview** — recommended 7-day outreach sequence
- **Affiliate tool links** — store your Loom, Descript, Instantly.ai, and GHL affiliate URLs

### Agency Tools
- **Skip Lead** — dismiss a contact without pushing, keep moving
- **Search web for lead** — open a Google search to find missing info
- **Batch push with shared tags and notes** — push 20 contacts with one click
- **Manual entry mode** — type in contact details manually while keeping scraped data

### White Label
- Custom **agency name** and **logo** in the sidebar
- Custom **brand color** — buttons, header, highlights all match your brand
- Custom **CRM display name** — rename "GHL" to anything ("Push to My CRM", "Push to ClientCRM")
- **Export/import settings** — onboard clients instantly with pre-configured JSON
- **Location profiles** — manage multiple GHL sub-accounts from one extension

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Install the Extension
Install LeadCapture Pro from the Chrome Web Store.

### Step 2: Connect GoHighLevel
1. Click the gear icon in the extension sidebar
2. Go to **GHL Connection** (Step 2 in settings)
3. Paste your **Private Integration API Key**
   - In GHL: Settings → Integrations → Private Integrations → Create new
   - Enable scopes: `contacts.readonly`, `contacts.write`, `opportunities.readonly`, `opportunities.write`, `locations.readonly`
4. Paste your **Location ID**
   - In GHL: Settings → Business Profile → scroll to bottom
5. Click **Test Connection** to verify

### Step 3: Set Your Defaults
In **Defaults** (Step 3):
- Pick a default pipeline and stage — saves time on every push
- Add default tags — e.g. `agency-lead`, `cold-outreach`

### Step 4: Start Capturing
1. Browse to any business directory or website
2. Click the **LeadCapture Pro** button in your browser toolbar
3. Review detected contacts in the sidebar
4. Set pipeline, stage, and tags
5. Click **Push to GHL**

The contact appears in your GHL account instantly.

## Troubleshooting

**400 error when pushing**
Usually means GHL rejected the data. The error banner shows GHL's exact message. Most common causes: email format invalid, or phone number less than 10 digits.

**Note isn't showing in GHL contact fields**
Notes appear under the **Activities** tab on the contact record — not in the main contact fields. Click Activities in the contact view.

**Address not detected on Google Maps**
Google Maps loads address data dynamically. Wait 2–3 seconds for the page to fully load, then open LeadCapture Pro. If still missing, click "Enter contact manually" — your scraped name, website, and address are preserved; only the phone/email fields switch to manual inputs.

**Phone confidence is yellow or red**
Yellow = found in page text (likely correct). Red = low confidence (verify before pushing). Green = from a tel: link or structured data (most reliable).

**Duplicate contact found**
Use **Update** to merge new data into the existing record, or **Skip** to dismiss and push anyway.
